Coffee vs. Energy Drinks

Which product category should a company promote—and when?

Business question

Should a company focus its next promotional campaign on coffee or energy drinks based on consumer search-interest data?

Recommendation: Test energy drink promotions in April–August and coffee promotions in October–March, rather than choosing a category based on average index values. Validate the index methodology and sales performance before making a large budget commitment.

Data-trust test

  1. Unverified scale: The AI initially displayed coffee's 59.61 average beside energy drinks' 40.92 average. Without index documentation, the difference cannot establish which category has greater absolute demand.
  2. Incomplete 2025: November and December 2025 are absent. Energy drinks typically score lower in those months, so the January–October 2025 average of 46.69 should not be compared directly with full-year averages.
  3. Correction: Compare January–October across all years for within-category trends; remove unsupported claims that coffee is more popular or that energy drink demand is definitively growing.

Decision and limitations

Run small, measurable seasonal tests, then compare actual conversions, revenue, and margin. Before scaling up, confirm the source and normalization of the search indices, update the dataset beyond October 2025, and obtain business performance data.

Confidence: Moderate in the reported seasonal pattern; low in any cross-category search-volume or sales conclusion.
